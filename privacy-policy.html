Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Quiktalk AI (Auckland, New Zealand)
Summary for Busy Business Owners:

Quiktalk AI builds 24/7 custom AI voice receptionists. We collect only the contact information necessary to configure your demo agent and provide phone-answering services. We never sell your personal data or your callers' data, and we operate under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and Australian Privacy Act 1988 frameworks.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information when you interact with our website, request a demonstration voice agent, or engage our services:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, business name, direct telephone number, email address, and company website URL.
  • Operational Inquiries: The types of calls your business receives (e.g. emergency callouts, appointment bookings, general triage), call volume estimates, and integration preferences (such as Fergus, Tradify, Cliniko, ServiceM8).
  • Call Recordings & Transcripts: When our AI voice agents answer calls on your behalf, audio streams and automated text transcripts are generated to execute appointments, log details to your CRM, and send SMS/email summaries.
  • Technical Data: Non-identifying technical metadata including browser type, device category, IP address, and interaction events via privacy-preserving local analytics.

2. Purpose and Lawful Basis for Processing

We collect and process your information in accordance with the New Zealand Information Privacy Principles (NZ Privacy Act 2020) and the Australian Privacy Principles (Privacy Act 1988):

  • To build, test, and deliver your personalized AI voice receptionist.
  • To schedule and execute demonstration calls with your consent.
  • To integrate with your existing calendars, POS systems, and CRMs upon your authorization.
  • To provide ongoing customer support, technical diagnostics, and system maintenance.
  • To communicate important service updates, billing statements, and optional product announcements.

3. Call Recording & Consent Disclosures

Quiktalk AI agents operate as telephone voice assistants for commercial businesses. Depending on your jurisdiction:

  • New Zealand: The Privacy Act 2020 requires organizations collecting personal information directly from individuals to make them aware that collection is taking place, the purpose, and intended recipients. Our AI agents state their nature upfront during greeting.
  • Australia: Under state and federal Surveillance Devices legislation, two-party consent may be required. We configure introductory disclosures (e.g. "Calls are answered by our automated voice assistant and recorded for quality and booking confirmation") according to your state regulations.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We implement technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information:

  • Data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS (Transport Layer Security 1.2+ and HTTP/3).
  • Database and storage access is restricted strictly to authorized operational staff.
  • Lead form data submitted for trial evaluations is retained for up to 90 days following evaluation unless you request earlier deletion or enter a commercial subscription.
  • Call transcripts and recordings for active clients are retained according to the retention window you configure in your service agreement.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third-party data brokers. We utilize reputable infrastructure providers strictly to execute telephony and computing services:

  • Cloud infrastructure and edge networking providers (e.g. Cloudflare).
  • Authorized carrier and VoIP telephony partners.
  • Your designated third-party software (e.g. Google Calendar, Outlook, ServiceM8, Tradify) exclusively through client-authorized API connections.

6. Your Rights Under NZ & AU Law

You have the right to request access to any personal information we hold about you and to ask for corrections if it is inaccurate. You may also request that we erase your demonstration data.

To exercise these rights, email our privacy team directly at admin@quiktalkai.com. We will respond within 20 working days in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act.
