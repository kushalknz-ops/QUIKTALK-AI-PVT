FAQ

Questions owners ask us every week.

What does Quiktalk AI do? +
Quiktalk AI is an AI voice receptionist service. It answers your business calls 24/7 in natural speech, asks the right questions, qualifies callers, books appointments into your calendar or CRM, and sends SMS confirmations. You keep your number; we handle the rest.
Will callers know they are talking to an AI? +
Our voice agents sound natural and polite. We configure each agent to disclose that it is an AI assistant where required by law or by your preference, and any complex call can be warm-transferred to your team in one tap.
Can I keep my existing business phone number? +
Yes. You keep your current number. Quiktalk AI uses conditional call forwarding for missed or busy calls, or full call forwarding from your carrier, so every call is answered day and night — no customer ever learns a new number.
How much does an AI receptionist cost? +
Pricing is a flat monthly rate based on your call volume — no per-minute surprises. Most businesses hear their custom demo agent before choosing a plan, and the demo is free with no credit card required. You'll get exact pricing on your demo call.
How long does setup take? +
Standard onboarding takes 2 to 3 business days. We build your agent's knowledge base, test real call scenarios with you, connect your calendar or CRM, and review test calls together before going live.
What happens on complex or emergency calls? +
You set the rules. Your agent can warm-transfer an emergency call straight to your mobile, or log an urgent SMS and email alert while reassuring the caller. Nothing urgent is left waiting for voicemail.
Does it book appointments into my calendar automatically? +
Yes. When a caller agrees to a time, the AI checks your live calendar, books the appointment, and sends an SMS confirmation with the details and a reschedule link — no double-booking, no paper notes.
Can I listen to calls or read transcripts? +
Yes. Every call is logged with a transcript and summary in your dashboard, so you can review what callers asked, how the agent responded, and which calls turned into booked jobs.
Which tools does it integrate with? +
We connect from day one with Google Calendar, HubSpot, Xero, MYOB, Stripe, Square, Twilio, Zapier and Make. For New Zealand and Australian job-management and practice tools — Fergus, Tradify, ServiceM8, SimPRO, Cliniko, Halaxy, Timely and Fresha — we wire up your specific system during onboarding, and most businesses go live with calendar booking and SMS confirmations first. Desktop clinic systems like D4W, EXACT, Medtech and Best Practice need per-practice setup — we'll confirm what's possible for yours before you sign.
Is it legal to answer and record calls this way? +
Quiktalk AI follows call-recording consent rules where they apply and can be configured to state it's an AI assistant at the start of the call. During onboarding we'll confirm the disclosure rule for your state or country.
Do I need new hardware or software? +
No. Quiktalk AI runs on your existing phone number and internet connection. There's no hardware to install, no desk phone, and nothing for your customers to download.
What if the AI can't answer a caller's question? +
Your agent is trained on your services, hours and pricing. Anything outside that triggers the rule you choose: a warm transfer to your team, or a message that you'll call the customer back — with their details already captured.
