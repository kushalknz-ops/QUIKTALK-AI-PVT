Why We Built Quiktalk AI

In Australia and New Zealand, 30% of all phone calls to trades and healthcare practices go unanswered. When a customer has an emergency roof leak, toothache, or vehicle breakdown, they don't leave a voicemail—they hang up and call the next business on Google.

Traditional answering services charge up to $2.50 per minute simply to write down a name and email you a message 20 minutes later. Meanwhile, hiring a full-time receptionist costs $55,000 to $65,000 NZD annually and still leaves nights, weekends, and lunch breaks completely uncovered.

Quiktalk AI was created to give small and mid-sized businesses the voice capabilities of an enterprise contact center for a fraction of the cost.

The 3-Business-Day Onboarding Roadmap

Day 1 — Knowledge Base & Prompt Engineering We review your services, operating areas, pricing guidelines, emergency protocols, and frequently asked questions.
Day 2 — Integration & Audio Simulation We connect your calendar or CRM (Tradify, Fergus, Cliniko, etc.) and conduct test calls with you so you can hear and adjust your agent's tone.
Day 3 — Number Activation & Go-Live You keep your existing phone number. We set up conditional call forwarding (so calls forward only when you're busy or after hours), and your agent begins handling calls.
